Preface / Introduction

David, the shepherd boy turned king, has always captured the hearts of believers not because he was perfect, but because he pursued God passionately.

As I studied David’s life, I discovered three divine patterns secrets that shaped him into a man after God’s own heart. These secrets are not hidden from us they’re hidden for us to discover, apply, and live by.

In this book, I invite you on a journey. You will see David as a son, a servant, a soldier, and a sovereign. But more than that you’ll discover how to become someone after God's own heart in your own life.

May you find strength as a warrior, intimacy as a worshipper, and authority as a king or queen in your divine calling.

- JeDiDiAh JsK
